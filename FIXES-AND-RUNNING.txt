TEMPORAL STABILIZATION
Rendering > Temporal stabilization is enabled by default and applies to every
ONNX interpolation model. Duplicate/nearly identical frame pairs bypass model
inference. Exact stationary pixels use the newer real frame; low-motion output
is clamped to the range of the two real endpoints with a small tolerance.
Eroded masks exclude moving-object boundaries, and clear motion remains under
model control. Disable the switch for a direct comparison. This targets the
observed real/generated shimmer while standing still; live game verification
is still required before changing the conservative thresholds.
